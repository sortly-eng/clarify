# Clarify by Sortly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Clarify skills plugin published in the `sortly-eng/clarify` repository. It does not describe the separate Sortly inventory application.

## What the plugin does

Clarify packages instructions that help an AI assistant turn feature ideas and bug reports into implementation briefs. The package contains no hosted backend, MCP server, account system, analytics, or telemetry code. Installing this package does not establish a connection to Sortly inventory data or send conversation content to a Clarify-operated service.

## Information used during a task

The assistant may use your prompts, answers, source code, designs, screenshots, logs, and documents that you provide or authorize it to access. These inputs help it ask questions, calculate requirement-completeness scores, and prepare a brief. The host application runs the assistant and controls access to files and connected tools.

Clarify itself does not maintain a separate database of conversations or inputs, sell those inputs, or use them for advertising. Conversation retention, model processing, training settings, and deletion are governed by the host application's policies and your account or workspace settings. Any connected service used during a task also has its own data practices. This policy does not change those practices.

## Support and public contributions

If you open an issue or pull request on GitHub, your GitHub identity and the information you submit are visible according to GitHub's settings. This repository is public. Maintainers can read and respond to those submissions. Do not post passwords, API keys, customer records, private source code, or other sensitive information in public issues.

## Your choices and contact

You can stop using or uninstall the plugin, limit the files and tools available to your assistant, and manage conversations through the host application's controls. For a Clarify-specific privacy question, use the [support instructions](SUPPORT.md). For deletion of host conversations or connected-service data, use that provider's privacy controls or support channel.

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
